Gym management software pricing varies considerably. Some products charge a recurring subscription, while others offer free plans with membership limits or different packages for growing businesses. For gym owners in India, understanding these pricing structures can help identify software that suits their current needs and future plans.

How Much Does Gym Management Software Cost in India?

The cost of gym management software depends on several factors, including:

  • Number of members
  • Number of branches
  • Available features
  • Payment and billing capabilities
  • Attendance and membership tracking
  • Reporting and export options
  • Data backup facilities
  • Subscription duration
  • Additional services or integrations

A small gym with 20 members may not need the same subscription as a multi-branch fitness business with hundreds of members. Instead of choosing software based on price alone, compare its capacity and features with your actual requirements.

What Features Should Be Included in Gym Software?

Member Management

The software should help you maintain member profiles, contact details and membership status.

Membership Tracking

Expiry tracking and renewal management help you identify members whose memberships are approaching their end dates.

Fee and Payment Management

Look for payment recording, member-wise balances, receipts and payment history.

Expense Tracking

Recording rent, salaries, utilities, repairs and other operating costs can help keep business records organised.

Attendance Management

If your gym records member visits, attendance features can help you maintain daily records.

Reports and Exports

Membership reports, payment summaries, outstanding balances and expense reports help you review business activity.

Branch Capacity

If you operate multiple locations, confirm that the selected subscription supports the number of branches you need.

Backup and Data Management

Check whether the software provides suitable options to back up and restore your records.

Which GymHQ Plan Is Right for Your Business?

1. Free Plan: ₹0

The Free plan supports up to 25 members and one branch. It is a starting point for small gyms and new fitness centres that want to organise their membership and payment records without paying a software subscription.

Best suited for: Small gyms and new businesses within the plan's capacity.

2. Starter Plan: ₹200 Per Month

The Starter plan supports up to 100 members and one branch. Its quarterly price is ₹550, while the yearly price is ₹2,000. At the monthly rate, twelve months would cost ₹2,400. The yearly plan therefore saves ₹400 compared with paying monthly for twelve months.

Best suited for: Independent gyms that need more membership capacity than the Free plan provides.

3. Growth Plan: ₹500 Per Month

The Growth plan supports up to 250 members and two branches. Its quarterly price is ₹1,400, and the yearly price is ₹5,000. Paying ₹500 each month for twelve months would cost ₹6,000. The yearly plan saves ₹1,000 compared with that monthly billing total.

Best suited for: Growing fitness centres and businesses managing up to two locations.

4. Pro Plan: ₹1,000 Per Month

The Pro plan supports up to 500 members and three branches. Its quarterly price is ₹2,750, while the yearly price is ₹10,000. Compared with paying ₹1,000 monthly for twelve months, the yearly option saves ₹2,000.

Best suited for: Established gyms that need greater member capacity or manage several branches.

5. Elite Plan: ₹2,000 Per Month

The Elite plan supports up to 1,000 members and five branches. Its quarterly price is ₹5,500, and its yearly price is ₹20,000. At the monthly rate, twelve months would cost ₹24,000. The yearly plan saves ₹4,000 compared with paying monthly for twelve months.

Best suited for: Larger gyms and multi-branch fitness businesses that need higher membership capacity.

How to Choose Gym Software Based on Member Count

Your current member count is a useful starting point when selecting a plan.

  • Up to 25 members: Consider the GymHQ Free plan.
  • 26–100 members: Consider Starter.
  • 101–250 members: Consider Growth.
  • 251–500 members: Consider Pro.
  • 501–1,000 members: Consider Elite.

These ranges are based on the listed membership limits. Your branch requirements may influence your choice as well. For example, a gym with 150 members and two branches would fit within the Growth plan's stated capacity. A gym with 150 members and three branches would need to consider a plan with a higher branch limit.

Is Cheap Gym Management Software Worth It?

Low pricing is useful, but the cheapest software is not always the best fit. Consider the time spent managing records manually, the features you actually use and the ease of finding payment and membership information.

A more useful comparison asks:

  • Can you find member details quickly?
  • Can you identify upcoming membership expirations?
  • Can you see outstanding balances?
  • Can you record and retrieve payment receipts?
  • Can you track expenses?
  • Can you export the reports you need?
  • Does the plan support your number of members and branches?
  • Can you back up and restore your records appropriately?

A reasonably priced solution that fits your workflow may be more useful than a cheaper product that lacks essential capabilities.
