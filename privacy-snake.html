We do not sell your personal information. We only share data when required by law or to comply with a legal obligation.

Your game data is stored locally on your device. We recommend keeping your device secure.

Our App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

You can delete the App at any time to remove all locally stored data. If you have questions about your data, contact us at admin@nbstudios.co.in.
