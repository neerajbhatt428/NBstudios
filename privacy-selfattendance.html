Privacy Policy

Self Attendance: Work & Study Effective: 20 Nov 2026

We have designed this App with a privacy-first, offline approach. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect or store your personal data on our servers.

01 Our Core Privacy Promise

  • No Accounts: We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any personal identifiers to use the App.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not have a backend server that collects your attendance data. All your profiles, timetables, attendance records, and notes are stored locally on your device using a secure local database (Room/SQLite).
  • No Tracking: We do not track your location, monitor your usage behavior, or sell your data to third parties.

02 Information Collection and Use

While we do not collect your data, the App generates and stores the following information locally on your device:

  • User-Provided Data: Profiles (College, Office), subjects, timetables, attendance marks (Present, Absent, Late, etc.), check-in/out times, overtime hours, notes, and deadlines.
  • App Preferences: Theme settings, language preferences, and app lock PINs (hashed and stored locally using Android's Keystore).

This data is used solely to provide the App's functionality (calculating attendance, generating reports, and sending local reminders).

03 Third-Party Services

Although we do not collect your data, the App integrates with third-party services provided by Google to support monetization and app functionality. These services may collect limited, non-personal data as governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): The free version of the App uses Google AdMob to display ads. We request non-personalized ads by default to protect your privacy. Google may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and interaction with ads. For users in regions covered by GDPR/CCPA, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather your consent.
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases): If you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is processed securely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your credit card information.
  • Google Drive / Cloud Backup (Optional): The App features an automatic backup system. If you choose to select a cloud folder (like Google Drive) via the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF), your backup file is sent directly to your own Google account. We do not have access to this file.

04 Permissions We Request

The App may request the following device permissions to function correctly:

  • Notifications: To send you daily summaries, class reminders, and backup alerts.
  • Biometric Hardware (Fingerprint/Face): To secure the App Lock feature. The biometric data never leaves your device.
  • Storage/File Access (SAF): To allow you to export PDF/Excel reports and save automatic backup files to your preferred folder.

05 Data Security & Encryption

We take the security of your local data seriously.

  • Encrypted Backups: If you set a password for your backup files, the file is encrypted using AES-256-GCM before being saved. The password is stored securely in your device's Android Keystore.
  • App Lock: Any PIN you set for the app lock is mathematically hashed (PBKDF2) and cannot be reversed.

06 Data Retention and Deletion

Because all your data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it.

  • You can delete individual attendance records, profiles, or backup files at any time within the App.
  • If you uninstall the App or clear the App's storage data from your Android system settings, all data will be permanently deleted. We cannot recover this data for you.

07 Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since the App stores all data locally and does not require a login, we do not collect any personal information from any user.

08 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
