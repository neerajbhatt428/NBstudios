About NB Studios

We are a one-developer studio. Every app is designed, written and supported by the same person, so feedback reaches the one who can act on it.

We build native Android apps for everyday needs, from small-business tools to games you can play in a few minutes.

  • Native Android, built for low-end and mid-range phones
  • Offline first, so apps work without a connection
  • No selling of personal data
  • Replies to every support email

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy applies to all apps published by NB Studios on Google Play.

Information we collect

Most of our apps store your data only on your device and do not send it to us. If an app asks for a permission, it is used only for the feature it names.

Third-party services

Some apps may use Google Play services, Firebase or Google AdMob for crash reports, analytics or ads. These services may collect device identifiers and usage data under their own privacy policies.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only when required by law.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can delete an app at any time to remove its local data. To ask about your data, email contact@nbstudios.co.in.
