Running a gym involves much more than helping members achieve their fitness goals. Gym owners also need to manage memberships, collect fees, track outstanding payments, monitor expenses, record attendance, handle renewals and maintain accurate financial records.

When these tasks are managed manually using notebooks, spreadsheets or multiple applications, it becomes difficult to keep everything organised. Missed renewal dates, forgotten payment reminders and incomplete expense records can create unnecessary work for gym owners.

GymHQ is a gym management software for Android designed to help gym owners manage their daily operations from their smartphones. From member registration and membership tracking to payment receipts, expense management and financial reports, GymHQ brings essential gym administration features together in one place.

Whether you run a small neighbourhood fitness centre, a growing gym or a multi-branch fitness business, GymHQ offers plans designed for different business sizes, including a free plan for gyms with up to 25 members.

What Is GymHQ Gym Management Software?

GymHQ is an Android-based gym management app that helps fitness business owners organise their daily administrative and financial activities.

Instead of maintaining separate records for members, membership fees, payments and expenses, you can use GymHQ to manage these records through dedicated sections within the app.

Its interface provides access to five main areas:

  • Home: View your gym's financial summary, member statistics, attendance and upcoming membership expirations.
  • Members: Add members, manage their profiles, check membership status and track outstanding balances.
  • Payments: Record member payments, view receipts and access payment history.
  • Expenses: Record business expenses, organise them by category and track spending.
  • Reports: Access reports covering memberships, collections, outstanding balances, expenses and financial ledgers.

GymHQ also includes settings for membership packages, batches, branches, reminders, app security, data backups and other business preferences. The objective is simple: help gym owners spend less time managing paperwork and more time focusing on their members and business.

Why Do Gym Owners Need Gym Management Software?

As a gym grows, keeping track of every member and transaction becomes increasingly complicated. Consider some common challenges faced by gym owners:

1. Keeping Track of Membership Expiry Dates

Members join on different dates and purchase different membership plans. Some memberships are monthly, while others are quarterly, half-yearly or annual. Manually checking every membership can make it easy to miss renewal dates.

GymHQ displays membership status and highlights memberships that are expiring soon. The dashboard includes an upcoming-expiry section, while the Members area helps you identify active, expired and soon-to-expire memberships. You can also configure the number of days used for expiring-soon alerts.

2. Tracking Pending Membership Fees

A member may pay the full membership fee upfront, make a partial payment or have an outstanding balance. Without a proper record, it can be difficult to determine who has paid and who still owes money.

GymHQ lets you record payments against memberships and view the amount due, amount paid and remaining balance. Member profiles also display payment information, making it easier to identify pending dues. The dashboard can show the outstanding amount owed by members, while the Reports section provides an outstanding-balances report.

3. Managing Gym Expenses

Rent, electricity, staff salaries, cleaning, equipment repairs, internet bills and other operating costs affect a gym's financial performance. Recording these expenses accurately is important for understanding where the business's money goes.

With GymHQ, you can record expenses with details such as Expense title and category, Amount and transaction date, Payment method, Vendor or payee, Invoice or reference number, Additional notes, and Receipt or invoice photograph. You can also organise expenses into categories and review category-wise spending.

4. Maintaining Attendance Records

Attendance tracking helps gym owners understand which members are visiting the facility and how frequently they attend. GymHQ includes an attendance feature that allows you to mark members as present and review their recent visit history. The member profile displays attendance information, and the Home screen provides a summary of the day's attendance.

5. Preparing Reports Without Manual Compilation

Compiling member lists, payment records and expense summaries manually can take considerable time. GymHQ includes a dedicated Reports section with multiple report types covering different aspects of gym operations, including Membership reports, Payment and collection reports, Expense and financial reports, and a Subscription and capacity report. Reports can be exported as CSV or PDF.

Key Features of GymHQ Gym Management App

1. Centralised Member Management

GymHQ provides a dedicated Members section for managing member information. You can add members, search by name, phone number or email, and filter records by membership status. Member profiles include contact information, membership plan and validity, status, amount paid and outstanding balance, attendance history, and payment-related actions.

2. Membership Packages and Renewals

GymHQ includes membership package management so you can configure reusable plans with fees and features. You can review membership validity, identify upcoming expirations and renew memberships through the member management workflow.

3. Payment Recording and Digital Receipts

GymHQ allows you to record member payments and maintain transaction details, including the payment date, payment method and reference information. The app also provides a receipt screen that displays details such as Receipt number, Payment amount, Member name, Associated membership, and Transaction reference. You can share receipts and attach a photograph of a payment slip.

4. Expense Tracking with Receipt Attachments

GymHQ is not limited to tracking membership income. It also helps you record the money your gym spends. Each expense can include its category, amount, date, payment method, vendor information and notes, as well as a photograph of a receipt.

5. Dashboard with Important Business Information

GymHQ's Home screen brings together several operational and financial indicators, including Revenue recorded during the selected period, Expenses, Net cash flow, Outstanding member balances, Total members, Active members, Members expiring soon, Today's attendance, and Members requiring reminders.

Important: Net cash flow in GymHQ is a cash-based summary of recorded transactions, not the same as accounting profit. Understanding this distinction helps you interpret the dashboard correctly.

6. Attendance and Batch Management

Different gyms operate different schedules, such as morning and evening sessions. GymHQ includes batch management for organising members into time slots. When attendance tracking is enabled, you can record daily visits and view recent attendance information in member profiles.

7. Reminders for Payments and Membership Renewals

GymHQ includes reminder-related features for payment dues, renewals and expired memberships. The app also provides message templates for welcome messages, reminders and thank-you messages.

8. Multi-Branch Gym Management

Gym owners planning to expand may need to manage multiple branches. GymHQ offers different subscription plans with varying branch limits. The app includes branch management settings for managing and switching between gym branches.

9. Data Backup and Recovery Options

GymHQ includes a Backup & Data section for backing up, restoring or deleting data. It also provides an automatic-backup setting. Owners should configure their backup preferences and periodically verify that backups are available and restorable.

10. Customisation and App Security

GymHQ provides settings that allow you to configure different aspects of your gym's operations. Available options include Gym name and contact details, Membership packages, Expense categories, Batches and branches, Expiring-soon alerts, Appearance preferences, App lock, Activity log, and Backup settings. The app also includes an activity log to help review recorded changes.
