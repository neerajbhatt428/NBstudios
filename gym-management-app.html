The Gym Owner's Dilemma

You didn't open a gym to spend your evenings staring at an Excel spreadsheet or chasing people for ₹1,500. You opened it because you love fitness and want to build a community.

But as your member count grows, the administrative chaos takes over:

1. The "Who Owes What?" Nightmare

Paper registers get lost, Excel sheets have errors, and you lose track of who has paid and whose membership expired. You're leaving money on the table.

2. The Trust Deficit with Staff

Is your front desk staff giving out free entries? Are they pocketing the cash for a day pass? Without a digital trail, you are completely in the dark.

3. The Cash Flow Blind Spot

You know money is coming in, but where is it going? Rent, salaries, equipment repairs, and electricity bills eat into your profits. If you don't track expenses, you can't grow.

Inside the App: 5 Features You'll Love

1. Flawless Member & Payment Tracking

GymHQ acts as a complete digital ledger. When a member pays, you generate a digital receipt instantly. You can log Cash, UPI, or Card payments. If you make a mistake, you don't just delete the record—you "Reverse" it, ensuring your books always balance. You can even send a quick "Thank You" message to the member via WhatsApp right from the receipt screen.

2. Zero-Chaos Expense Management

Stop stuffing paper bills into a drawer. With GymHQ's Expense tracker, you can log rent, staff salaries, or even ₹600 for drinking water cans. You can categorize expenses, attach a photo of the invoice, and instantly see a visual breakdown of where your money is going.

3. Smart Attendance & Expiry Alerts

Never let an expired member slip through the cracks. The dashboard shows you exactly who is "Expiring soon" and who hasn't shown up in a while. At the front desk, marking a member "Present" takes one tap. Your staff gets limited access, so they can check people in without seeing your private financial reports.

4. Powerful Business Reports

Need to know your exact profit margin? Want to see which membership plan is the most popular? GymHQ includes a massive suite of reports—from Income vs. Expenses to Outstanding Balances and Member-wise payment histories. You can export any report as a CSV or PDF for your accountant.

5. Complete Privacy & Automated Backups

Because GymHQ doesn't rely on a cloud server, your data is 100% private. You can lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint. And to ensure you never lose your data if you switch phones, GymHQ features Automatic Backups—saving your entire database to Google Drive or your phone's storage daily, weekly, or monthly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does GymHQ require an internet connection?

No. GymHQ is built offline-first. You can manage members, collect payments, and check people in even if your Wi-Fi goes down. The internet is only needed if you choose to back up your data to the cloud.

Is my members' data safe?

Absolutely. Your data is stored securely on your device. We don't sync your members' personal data to our servers. You can also enable a PIN or biometric lock to keep the app secure.

Can my front desk staff use it?

Yes. You can set up an App Lock and assign limited staff access. They can check members in and mark payments received, but they won't be able to view your overall revenue, expenses, or profit reports.

How do backups work?

You can configure GymHQ to automatically save a secure backup file to your Google Drive, SD card, or local storage at a time you choose (e.g., 11:00 PM daily). You can also do manual backups any time.
