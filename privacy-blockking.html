01 Our Commitment to Children's Privacy

Block King is designed to be a safe, fun environment for children. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the US, the GDPR-K in the EU/UK, and Google Play's Families Policy.

We do not collect, request, or store any personal information from children or any user. There are no accounts, no login screens, and no text inputs that ask for personal details.

02 Information Collection and Use

We do not collect personal data. Any data generated by the game is stored locally on your device:

  • Game Progress: Your high scores and unlocked levels are saved locally using Android DataStore. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.
  • Settings: Any preferences (like sound or theme choices) are saved locally.

03 Advertising (Child-Directed)

Block King is free to play and supported by advertisements served by Google AdMob.

  • Family-Safe Ads: We have configured our app to show only ads that are appropriate for children.
  • No Personalized Ads: We have tagged our app for "child-directed treatment." This means Google AdMob is instructed not to use your child's Advertising ID or location to serve personalized ads. The ads shown will be contextual and non-personalized.
  • No Tracking: AdMob may still collect limited diagnostic data to ensure ads load properly, but this is strictly governed by Google's Families Policy.

For more information, please review Google's Family Policy requirements here: AdMob Families Policy.

04 Parental Rights

As a parent or guardian, you have the right to review, delete, or refuse further collection of your child's data. Since we do not collect any personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete.

To delete all game data (including high scores and settings), you can simply clear the app's storage in your Android device settings or uninstall the app.

If you believe your child has somehow provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete it.

05 Data Security

Because we do not transmit or store your data on external servers, the security of your data relies on the security of your own device. We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock.

06 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage parents to review this page periodically.
