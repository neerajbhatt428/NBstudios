01 About this policy

This policy explains how the GymHQ Android app (package com.neeraj.GymHQ) handles information. GymHQ is published by Neeraj Bhatt / NB Studios ("we", "us"). The app is used by gym owners and their staff ("you") to manage members, memberships, payments, expenses, attendance and reports. Your gym's members do not use the app.

GymHQ works offline. It has no user accounts, no cloud sync, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

02 Information you enter, and where it stays

You may enter:

  • About your gym: gym name, branches, address, phone numbers, email, packages, batches and message templates.
  • About your members: name, mobile number, and optionally email, address, emergency contact, photo, notes, batch, membership dates, fees, payments, refunds, freezes and attendance.
  • About your business: expenses, vendors, invoice numbers and receipt photos.
  • Activity log: a record of actions taken in the app (for example "payment reversed"), with whether the owner or staff did it.

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We never receive it. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this data.

You decide what to record about your members. You are responsible for having a lawful reason to record their information and for telling them how you use it.

03 What leaves your phone

Only the following, and only when you use the feature.

WhenWhat is sentTo whomWhy
You buy or restore a subscription Google Play purchase token and a random ID created for this app installation Our subscription server (Cloudflare), over HTTPS To confirm the subscription with Google and keep it active on one phone at a time
You buy a subscription Payment details Google Play only Google processes the payment under its own terms. We never see your card or UPI details
You send a message to a member The message text and the member's number WhatsApp or SMS app you choose The app opens that app with the message ready; you press send
You share or save a report, receipt or backup The file you chose The place you pick (Drive, WhatsApp, a folder) Exporting is always your choice

Our server stores only a one-way hash of the purchase token, the installation ID, the plan and its expiry, and a count of phone moves. No member, payment, expense or other gym data is ever sent to us.

Backup files contain all your gym data. Keep them somewhere private. We do not sell, rent or share any information with advertisers or data brokers.

04 Phone permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
Contacts (optional) Only when you choose to import several contacts as members. Only the contacts you confirm are saved, on the phone. Picking a single contact uses Android's picker and needs no permission.
Biometrics (optional) To unlock the app with your fingerprint or face if you turn on App lock. The fingerprint stays with Android; the app never receives it.
Internet Only to check your subscription with our server and Google Play.

Photos and receipts are chosen with Android's photo picker, which shares only the images you select. The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone or full storage access.

If you set an App lock or staff PIN, only a salted hash of it is stored on the phone.

05 Security

Data on the phone is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add an App lock with a PIN, password or fingerprint, and a separate staff PIN with limited access. All communication with our server uses HTTPS.

06 Keeping and deleting data

  • On your phone: data stays until you delete it. Settings → Backup & data → Delete all data removes every record, photo, receipt and setting. Uninstalling the app also removes them. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected.
  • On our server: the subscription record is kept while your subscription is active and for up to 12 months after, for fraud prevention. Email us to have it deleted sooner.

07 Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you (only the subscription record described above) and raise a grievance. We reply within 30 days. Your gym's members should contact the gym directly, since only the gym holds their data.

08 Children

GymHQ is meant for business owners and is not directed at children under 18.

09 Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
