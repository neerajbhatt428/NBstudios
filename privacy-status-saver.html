Status Saver 2026 - Video Download

This policy explains what the Status Saver 2026 - Video Download app does with your information — and, more importantly, what it does not do.

Last updated: 1 January 2026 · Effective from the same date

NB Studios ("we", "us", "our") develops the Android application Status Saver 2026 - Video Download ("the app"). This privacy policy applies to that app and to this web page. It does not apply to any other app or service.

By installing or using the app, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

The short version

  • The app works entirely on your device. We do not run a server and we never receive your photos, videos or statuses.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or any kind of account or login.
  • The app needs media/storage permission so it can read the statuses you have already viewed on your own phone and copy the ones you choose into your gallery.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and similar technical data to serve and measure ads. This is the only third-party data collection in the app.
  • We do not sell your personal data. Ever.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on your device.

1. Who we are

NB Studios is an independent Android developer based in India. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at admin@nbstudios.co.in.

2. Information we do not collect

To be completely clear, the app does not collect, request or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Any account, login or sign-up details
  • Your chats, messages, call logs or contact list
  • Your precise location (GPS)
  • Your camera, microphone or files that are unrelated to statuses
  • Your photos, videos or statuses — nothing you view or save in the app is uploaded to us or to anyone else

3. Information processed on your device

Everything the app does with your files happens locally, on your phone.

Reading statuses

The app reads the folder on your device's storage where your messaging app keeps the statuses you have already viewed. It displays those files in a grid so you can choose which ones to keep. The app cannot see statuses you have not viewed, and it does not connect to any messaging service, server or account.

Saving statuses

When you tap save, the app copies the file you selected into your own gallery (for example, a Status Saver 2026 folder inside your Pictures or Movies directory). That copy belongs to you and stays on your device.

The 7-day history feature

If you use the 7-day history feature, the app keeps a copy of statuses it has processed inside its own private storage for up to seven days, so you can still save something after the original status has expired. These copies live only on your device. They are deleted when you clear the history inside the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

App settings

The app stores a small amount of preference data (such as your theme choice or history setting) locally on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere.

5. Advertising

The app is free and is supported by advertising. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK).

When ads appear

Full-screen "interstitial" ads may be shown at natural breaks in use — for example, after you save several statuses in one go, or from time to time while saving statuses one by one. Ads are never shown over a button you are about to tap, and they can always be closed immediately.

What the advertising SDK may collect

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent identifier)
  • Your IP address, from which a general (city-level) location may be inferred
  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • How you interact with ads, such as impressions, clicks and views
  • Diagnostic and fraud-prevention signals

This information is used to display ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ad performance, and to detect invalid or fraudulent traffic. Google acts as an independent controller for much of this processing.

Your choices

  • Consent prompt: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a Google-certified consent message before any personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings or menu.
  • Turn off personalised ads: on your device, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording may vary by manufacturer) and select Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalisation. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Reset your advertising ID: the same settings screen lets you reset the identifier at any time.

We do not receive your personal details from Google. We may see aggregate, anonymised reports such as total impressions and revenue. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

The main risk to the files you save is the same as for any other file on your phone: someone else gaining access to your unlocked device. We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock.
